🔑 Science Clues Dictionary

Mixture: Two or more materials mixed together that can be physically separated.

Retain: To continue to keep something (like a material keeping its own property).

Solute: A solid that dissolves in a liquid (e.g., salt or sugar).

Solvent: The liquid in which a solid dissolves (e.g., water).

📝 Video Explanation Breakdown:

🍬 Jerry’s Sugar Experiment: Cup 1 (Sugar + Water) vs. Cup 2 (Sugar + Salt)

• Cup 1: Sugar dissolves in water and seems to disappear, but the drink still tastes sweet! Sugar is soluble in water.
• Cup 2: Mixed with salt, sugar still looks and feels the same! The properties of sugar stay the same after mixing.

🧂 LESSON 3: SOLUBLE VS. INSOLUBLE SOLIDS

Where Did the 5g of Salt Go?! 🔍💧

A solid that can dissolve in a liquid to form a solid/liquid mixture is called a solute. The liquid it dissolves in is called a solvent (like water).

⚖️ Mass Check: Water + 5g Salt = +5g Heavier!
🧪 WHY DOES SALT SEEM TO DISAPPEAR?

Salt is a Soluble Solid (Solute)!

Salt dissolves in water and seems to disappear. After adding 5 g of salt to water, the water becomes 5 g heavier! This proves the salt is still in the water, broken into pieces too tiny to see.

🚫 Insoluble Solids: Sand cannot dissolve in water — sand is an insoluble solid and stays visible!
🕸️ SEPARATION METHOD 1: SIEVING BY SIZE

Separating Fine Salt, Rice, Lentils & Penne!

Size order from Smallest to Biggest: Fine salt ➔ Rice grains ➔ Lentils ➔ Penne.

📏 Sieve Hole Rule:

• Solids with smaller sizes than the sieve holes can pass through.

• Solids with bigger sizes than the sieve holes cannot pass through (they stay on top!).

🪜 Step-by-Step Sieve Order (Biggest Holes ➔ Smaller Holes):

Step 1 (Sieve 1 — biggest holes): Left on sieve = Penne; passes through = rice, lentils & salt.

Step 2 (Sieve 2): Left on sieve = Lentils; passes through = rice & salt.

Step 3 (Sieve 3): Left on sieve = Rice grains; passes through = Fine salt.

🧲 SEPARATION METHOD 2: MAGNETS (SAFETY FIRST!)

Iron Thumbtacks, Steel Clips & Joe’s Sharp Nails Box!

⚠️ Safety Rule: Do NOT reach into sharp nails or iron thumbtacks with bare hands! A thumbtack and a lentil have similar sizes, so a sieve cannot separate them — use a magnet because iron is magnetic and attracted to a magnet!
📦 Joe’s Box (Sand + Sharp Iron Nails + Stones):
• Step 1: Use a magnet to pull out the sharp iron nails safely (iron is magnetic).
• Step 2: Use a sieve to separate the stones from the sand because stones and sand have different sizes!

🫗 Separation Method 3: Insoluble Solid + Liquid Through a Sieve!

🪨 + 💧 Stones and Water

• Left on the sieve: Stones (too big for the holes!)

• Pass through the sieve: Water

⏳ + 💧 Sand and Water (Wide Sieve)

• Left on wide sieve: None (sand grains are tiny!)

• Pass through wide sieve: Both sand and water pass through together!
